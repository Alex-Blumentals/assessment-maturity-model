# Fellowship papers — AI-literacy competence measurement

Two companion papers produced under the **StandICT.eu 2029 Fellowship** (ID 2029-02-1741), contributed toward CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 through Latvia's national mirror committee **LVS/STK 59**. Both are licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

| Paper | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**The Unmeasured Obligation**](the-unmeasured-obligation.md) | A neutral, product-blind gap analysis: no Article-4-native, deployer-workforce competence-measurement standard exists, and the properties one must have. |
| [**A Candidate Basis for an AI-Literacy Competence Standard**](twin-ladder-candidate-basis.md) | An interest-declared contribution paper mapping the Twin Ladder methodology (this repository's standard) against those properties. |

Published October 2026, open for comment. Companions to the [Twin Ladder Assessment Maturity Model](../STANDARD.md) in this repository.

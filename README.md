# Twin Ladder Assessment Maturity Model v1.0

**The open standard for AI competence assessment under EU AI Act Article 4.**

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC_BY--SA_4.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

## What is this?

A six-pillar maturity model for assessing organisational AI competence, designed to operationalise the EU AI Act Article 4 obligation. The model provides:

- **6 assessment pillars**: Awareness, Policy & Data Protection, Training, Tools, Evidence, Governance
- **4 maturity levels**: Exploring (0-25), Developing (26-50), Implementing (51-75), Optimizing (76-100)
- **Article 4 compliance floor** at approximately score 52
- **GDPR integration** for data protection in AI contexts
- **Cross-pillar dependency analysis** for holistic assessment

## Why this standard?

No existing framework is simultaneously:
1. **Competence-specific** — centres on human capability, not governance processes
2. **Article 4-native** — built for the EU AI Act's competence obligation
3. **Open-source** — free to adopt, adapt, and redistribute (CC BY-SA 4.0)
4. **Individual + organisational** — assesses both people and institutions
5. **Workflow-based** — measures practical competence, not technical knowledge

See the [competitive landscape analysis](https://twinladder.ai/en/research/assessment-maturity-model) for detailed positioning against 18 existing frameworks.

## How to use

**For organisations:** Use the pillar definitions and maturity indicators to self-assess your AI competence readiness. The compliance floor (~52) indicates the minimum level for Article 4 compliance.

**For assessors:** Use the scoring methodology and rubric to conduct standardised assessments. The CC BY-SA 4.0 license permits commercial use with attribution and share-alike.

**For automated assessment:** Visit [twinladder.ai/assess](https://twinladder.ai/assess) for an AI-powered assessment that scores your organisation against this standard in 15 minutes.
